Quiz Rush

Fastest wins

Timed multiple choice. Every player or team locks in an answer on the shared screen. Faster correct answers score more, up to 1,000 points, plus a bonus for answer streaks.

Japarty Board

Classic

Six categories, five clues each. The player or team in control picks a clue, then everyone locks in an answer and right answers earn the clue's value. One hidden Daily Double lets someone bet big, and Final Japarty ends it with typed answers and wagers. Also works in live games on phones.

True or False Blitz

Warm-up

Ten-second rounds of true or false statements. Quick, loud, and good for getting a room going before the main game.

Timeline Shuffle

Take turns

Players or teams take turns putting four historic moments in order, earliest first, before the clock runs out. 250 points per correct spot and a 500-point bonus for a perfect timeline.

Guess the Song

Music round

A short clip plays and everyone picks the song title from four choices before time runs out. Add your own song files, or start with the two public-domain starter tunes Japarty plays itself. Also works in live games on phones.

Cash or Case

Take a chance

Each player or team picks a closed case, then opens the others round by round. After every round the Banker makes an offer: take the cash, or keep playing and hope your case holds more. A Black History question before each offer raises it 10% for a right answer. Also works in live games, where everyone plays their own case on their phone.

Who Am I?

Clue by clue

Four clues about a famous figure appear one at a time, hardest first. Lock in early for 1,000 points, or wait for an easier clue and earn less. Also works in live games.

Map Round

Tap the place

Where did it happen? Drop a pin on the map of the United States. The closer you are, the more points, up to 1,000 for a bullseye. Players take turns on one screen, or everyone taps their own phone in a live game.

    Import players

    Paste one name per line, or copy two columns from Excel or Google Sheets: Name and Team. You can also load a .csv or .txt file. Players then tap their own name on their phone instead of typing a nickname, and a Team column puts them on teams automatically.

    The list is saved on this computer for your next game. Results downloads include everyone on the list, with who didn't join.

    Let anyone join, no account needed

    1. Create a free project at supabase.com. No tables or setup inside it are needed.
    2. In the project, open Project Settings, then API Keys. Copy the Project URL and the anon or publishable key.
    3. Paste both into Japarty.html: either below, or into the line const LIVE_SERVER={url:"",key:""}; near the top of the file so every copy is ready.
    4. Put Japarty.html on a website, for example by dragging it onto app.netlify.com/drop, or with GitHub Pages or your school or company site.
    5. Open the file from that website on the big screen and choose Host a live game. Players scan the QR code. No sign-in, no app.

    The free Supabase plan handles about 200 phones at once. The key is designed to be public, and games last only while the host screen is open. Free projects pause after a week without use, so open the Supabase dashboard the day before an event to wake yours up.

    Guess the Song songs

    Add song files from this computer, then check each title. Players pick the right title from four choices, so short, clear titles work best. Songs are saved in this browser on this computer only, so set them up on the computer that runs the big screen. Only use music you have the right to play at your event.
